# Base Zombies

Modular gameplay enhancements layered onto the vanilla four-map Nazi Zombies experience, designed to stand on their own and eventually compose into Zombie Gauntlet.

## Language

### Structure

**Base Experience**:
The core single-map Nazi Zombies survival loop across the four Stock Maps before layering on standalone progression modes such as Zombie Gauntlet.
_Avoid_: Vanilla loop, Standard game, Regular zombies

**Enhancement**:
A scoped, modular gameplay improvement added to the Base Experience that can be reasoned about, tested, and toggled independently before composing with other mods.
_Avoid_: Tweak, Mini-mod, Addon, Patch

**Station**:
A physical in-world interactive terminal or kiosk spawned at curated coordinates on a Stock Map where players purchase perks, Classes, upgrades, or Companions.
_Avoid_: Vendor, Menu shop, Console command

### Perks & Classes

**Standard Perk**:
A core perk purchased during a match and lost when downed (the four stock perks plus Stamin-Up, Mule Kick, and Armor Piercing).
_Avoid_: Normal perk, Basic perk

**Perk Tier 2**:
A second-level upgrade applied to an already-owned Standard Perk, enhancing its effect until the player goes down.
_Avoid_: Super perk, Pro perk, Upgraded perk

**Apex Perk**:
A high-cost (8,000-point) late-game perk granting a powerful passive or survivability effect (Double Ammo, Lightweight Heavy, Second Chance, Blast Shield).
_Avoid_: Expensive perk, Mega perk, Ultra perk

**Class**:
A 5,000-point permanent-until-swapped player archetype (one active per player, never lost on down, swappable at a Station): Field Medic, Titan, Sapper, Daredevil, Assassin, or Broker.
_Avoid_: Class perk, Role, Loadout

### Weapons, Upgrades & Squad

**Double Pack-a-Punch**:
A second tier of Pack-a-Punch applied to an already Pack-a-Punched weapon for an additional stat and ammo upgrade (separate from Elemental Weapons).
_Avoid_: Overclock, AAT, Elemental PaP

**Elemental Weapon**:
A wonder-tier weapon embodying one of the four elements—Fire (Enhanced Flamethrower), Electricity (Wunderwaffe DG-2), Acid (Acid Sprayer), or Frost (Cryo Cannon).
_Avoid_: Element attachment, Elemental ammo

**Special Weapon**:
A non-elemental bespoke weapon or tactical gear item expanding combat roles beyond standard firearms (War Club, Multi-Crossbow, Blade Boomerang, Portal Grenade).
_Avoid_: Wonder weapon, Gadget, Bonus gun

**Melee Slot**:
The single active close-quarters melee loadout carried by a player (Combat Knife, Bowie Knife, or War Club), swappable without consuming a primary weapon slot.
_Avoid_: Knife perk, Secondary melee, Melee weapon slot

**Companion**:
A hireable AI squad ally that follows its owner, keeps purchased weapons permanently, feeds kill points to its owner, respawns each round if killed, and never blocks player movement or gunfire (capped at 4 total squad members including human players).
_Avoid_: Bot, Mercenary, Turret
